### Known findings (Lane C — recorded, no action owed)
- The lift surface was HEALTHIER than this plan assumed, and both of its premises were phantom
  gaps (the 4-phantom-gaps pattern, again): `wordwrap`'s empty `lift_from` is a documented RULING
  (its semantics deliberately differ from PHP's on multi-byte input, so lifting one onto the other
  would silently change a lifted program), and `soundex` already carried `lift_from: &["soundex"]`.
- Of 309 rows with an empty `lift_from`, 242 emit a `__phorj_*` helper — correct by construction,
  since a helper exists precisely when PHP has no faithful counterpart to lift FROM. Of the 67
  emitting a core PHP call, only 6 do so in the exact `f(a0..an)` shape a lift needs, and 5 of those
  are blocked by the uniqueness invariant. `array_slice` was the single genuine opening.

### Known findings (Lane B — the ratchet, before the fixtures)
- `scripts/surface-ratchet.sh` had three blind spots, each found only because a number failed to
  move when it should have: it scanned `src/cli/explain/` as emit sites (so tombstones counted as
  unpayable debt); it recognised only the `"E-FOO"` form, missing the loader's 25 `[E-FOO]` codes
  and the transpiler's 3 `"E-FOO: …"` prefixes (they were being counted only via the catalog); and
  `lsp_providers` matched a shape that cannot occur in `INITIALIZE_RESULT`, so it counted provider
  names that TESTS quoted. Three tombstones fell out — `E-MODULE-UNAVAILABLE` (DEC-273),
  `E-VENDOR-MISSING` (folded into `E-MODULE-NOT-FOUND`; ADR 0005 still named it), `E-DECIMAL-DIV`
  (retired when bare decimal `/` was ruled legal; its two "assertions" are negative and pass forever).
- The corrected denominator is 311 and the gate's own suite grew from 19 to 23 cases, one per
  blind spot, so none can return silently.

### Known findings (Lane E)
- The DEC-431 B fix needed no new `Op`: the `TakeLocal` shape DEC-463 named was the ruled MEANS, and
  a one-op lookahead at `Concat(2)` reaches the same uniqueness with Invariant 3's exhaustive matches
  untouched. Remaining VM cost (18 ms vs the JIT's ~2 ms) is loop dispatch plus the per-line
  interpolation `Concat(k)` allocation — a separate, smaller item. DEC-431 A (the JIT cliff) is
  untouched and still pending its ruling.
